# M6 acceptance: video editor, tier 2

Status of the M6 acceptance criteria (roadmap: "VID-3 to VID-13; preview vs export audio mix parity within tolerance;
dropped frames under 2% on the reference project"), on 2026-11-19. Each row names what proves the requirement today and,
separately, what does not. Test names are the `it(...)` titles; files are under `packages/` unless marked `e2e/`.

Legend: **done** = built and tested at the level stated; **partly** = built, with a named gap; **open** = not finished.

## Cross-cutting acceptance

| Criterion | Status | Evidence | Gaps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preview vs export audio mix within tolerance | done, with a stated limit | `apps/web/src/app/project/video/audio-parity.test.ts`: 11 cases (plain, volume, track volume, fades, solo and mute, speed 0.5/2/4, everything together, seek into a clip with decoder buffers of 21 ms, 0.5 s and 1.37 s, summing and limiting). Tolerance asserted: **1e-3 per sample**; measured worst difference: below 1e-4 in every case (half speed is the largest, from sample interpolation, above 1e-5). | The live side is simulated with the real `audioSegments`, `placeBuffer` and `applyGain` and a model of Web Audio (straight-line resampling and gain curve). Not covered: the browser's own resampler, decoder buffer sizes and scheduling jitter. A real-browser comparison of `AudioContext` output with the export is not done. |
| Dropped frames under 2% on the reference project | done on one machine | `docs/engineering/m6-playback-measurement.md`, `spikes/m6-playback/run.mjs`: 1080p30, two video layers blended, a cross-fade, a title and sound; three runs of 23 s; **worst 0.43%, then 0.14% and 0%** (limit 2%). | One machine (Intel Core i7-1360P, Iris Xe, Chrome 157), synthetic test-pattern footage, 23 s, three runs; first run (cold) is the worst. Recorded, not gated: timing varies with the machine. Hardware decode use, late frames and sound dropouts are not measured. |
| Undo and redo in e2e editing scenarios | done | undo is asserted in the marker, speed, transition, text, look and handle tests listed above; `core/video-commands.test.ts` property test (random edits, undo exact) | No e2e that edits for a long time and undoes everything back to the start. |
| Files from older versions open | done | each format step has "a vN file still opens" (v5 to v9 in `core/*.test.ts`) | v10 and v11 are covered by the current tests of their own slices. |

## What this means for step 7

- Met with a named gap to record in the limitations: **VID-3** (no 8 + 8 track test), **VID-10** (waveform drawing), **VID-11** (colour never checked on pixels).
- A cheap way to close the three gaps before merging step 7: one e2e that adds 8 video and 8 audio tracks and checks the timeline stays usable; one e2e that sets brightness on a red clip and reads the preview pixel; one waveform smoke check that the canvas is not blank for a clip with sound.
